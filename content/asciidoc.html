FORMAT 02

AsciiDoc, checkpointed.

Useful conversion has landed through AD0-AD5 against immutable Eclipse language and TCK development snapshots. The upstream standard and TCK are still unreleased, so this is measured progress rather than a premature conformance claim.

Document structure

  • Titles and author/revision metadata
  • Attribute assignment and unsetting
  • Nested sections
  • Paragraph and block precedence
  • Source-positioned neutral nodes

Blocks and lists

  • Listing, literal and source blocks
  • Open, example and sidebar containers
  • Quotes, verse and comments
  • Ordered, unordered and description lists
  • Nested checklists and continuations

Inline substitutions

  • Strong, emphasis and monospace
  • Constrained and unconstrained forms
  • Mark, superscript and subscript
  • Escaping and replacements
  • Explicit line breaks

CURRENT BOUNDARY

Implemented, not overstated.

Links, media, tables, macros, includes, resource policies and the final ASG-based TCK gate remain in AD6-AD11. Asciidoctor compatibility is a separate future evidence track.
